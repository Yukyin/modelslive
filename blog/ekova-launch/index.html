Ekova Is Now Live

Yuyan (Yolanda) Chen · October 28, 2026

Ekova is now officially live. Open https://ekova.modelslive.org to start using it.

Ekova is made of five distinct personalities. Give them one situation, and each responds from its own angle.

Why we built Ekova

Everyone gets stuck sometimes. We ask friends, family, even AI, and the answers often sound the same. Most AI today responds in one uniform voice, while the same story told to five different friends brings back five different reactions. Those differences are what change the way a person sees the situation.

What Ekova does for you

First, it gives you five angles on one situation. Warm catches you gently and empathizes. Tsukkomi, playful but sharp, cuts to the core. Gonzo draws an analogy from a completely different field. Real faces reality head-on, anchored in constraints and facts. Coach shows you the simplest next step.

Second, it shows you the side you could not see. You read one situation through five perspectives at once. The value lies in the distance between them, because that distance is what shifts your own view.

Third, it leaves the decision with you. Ekova offers directions and leaves your life in your own hands. It works more like a mirror than a companion: the goal is for you to see more clearly on your own, not to depend on it.

How to use it

Ekova opens at set times each day, as shown on the page. Choose English or Chinese, describe what is on your mind, and all five personalities reply. Each conversation stands on its own in this public version.

Try it: https://ekova.modelslive.org

This is just the beginning. A customized Ekova remembers who you are and what matters to you, so every answer understands you better.

The friend who gets you, from every angle.
