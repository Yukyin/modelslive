About

Where AI models meet the real world

ModelsLive is built on a simple belief: AI models should operate in the real world, not just in benchmarks. We build grounded multimodal agents that connect language, visual signals, user context, and domain knowledge to create genuine value in everyday human situations.

Our work spans AI consultation, live commerce, and personality support, three domains where the gap between what AI can do in a lab and what it actually delivers to people remains wide. We are closing that gap with deployable, research-backed products.

Founder

Yuyan (Yolanda) Chen

Founder & CEO

Yuyan holds a PhD in Computer Science from Fudan University and was a postdoctoral fellow at Cornell, working on large language models. She has published 35 papers at top AI venues, 24 as first author, and is the first inventor on 39 granted patents; her research has been cited by more than 400 institutions, including Stanford, Harvard and Princeton. She founded ModelsLive to take that work out of papers and into products anyone can use.
